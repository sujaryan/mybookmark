Quick questions

Things readers usually ask.

Who is mybookmark for?

Everyone who reads. Kids, students, working professionals, retirees, families, book clubs. Our collection spans literary fiction, popular non-fiction, biographies, business and self-help, sci-fi and mystery, picture books, young-adult, and regional language titles. Tell us your taste and we'll find your sweet spot.

Where in Bengaluru do you deliver?

We currently serve Bengaluru-wide via batched twice-weekly door delivery. Delivery charges apply depending on your location. Enter your locality during signup and we'll confirm coverage.

How do I choose books? Do I have to browse or do you pick?

It's your call. Browse our catalogue and pick exactly what you want, or request any book we don't have and we'll source it within a week.

What if you don't have a book I want?

Request it. Use the "Request a book" feature in your dashboard - enter the title and author, and we'll source most books within a week and add them to your next delivery. If a book is rare or out of print and we genuinely can't get it, we'll let you know and suggest close alternatives. The catalogue grows because of what members ask for.

Can I see your catalogue before subscribing?

Yes. Create a free account and browse the full catalogue by genre, author, or reading level before picking a plan. No payment needed to browse.

What if a book gets damaged?

Books get read - we expect some wear and tear, and that's built into our model. For minor damage (small marks, slight bending), no charge. For serious damage (torn pages, lost books, water damage), we charge 50% of the cover price. We tell you upfront so there are no surprises.

How does the refundable deposit work?

The deposit is held as security against the books in your possession. It's not income - it's protection. When you cancel, we audit the returned books and refund the deposit within 7 working days.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime. Quarterly and annual plans are pro-rated on cancellation with a small ₹500 admin fee. Your deposit is always returned in full.

Do you have books in languages other than English?

Currently our collection is primarily English, with a small Hindi and Kannada selection growing month by month. If you'd like books in a specific language, mention it during signup - your input shapes what we buy next.

Need help?

Have questions? Reach out.

Our team is available to help with any queries about plans, delivery, or your account.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay a security deposit?

Yes, a refundable security deposit is required at the time of delivery. The exact amount is shared over WhatsApp. It's fully refunded when you return the console in good condition.

What games are available?

Every rental comes with a PS Plus subscription, giving you access to a library of 100+ games including God of War Ragnarok, Spider-Man 2, Hogwarts Legacy, and more. No need to buy individual games.

Can I extend my rental?

Absolutely. Just message us on WhatsApp before your rental period ends and we'll extend it seamlessly.

What if something breaks?

Normal wear and tear is on us. For accidental damage, the security deposit covers repairs. We'll walk you through the specifics at the time of delivery.

Where do you deliver?

All across Bengaluru. Same service area as our book library - if we deliver books to your area, we deliver the PS5 too.

How does the security deposit work?

A refundable deposit is collected at the time of delivery. Once you return the console in good condition, the full deposit is refunded within 2-3 business days.
