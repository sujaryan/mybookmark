Quick questions

Things readers usually ask.

Who is mybookmark for?

Everyone who reads. Kids, students, working professionals, retirees, families, book clubs. Our collection spans literary fiction, popular non-fiction, biographies, business and self-help, sci-fi and mystery, picture books, young-adult, and regional language titles. Tell us your taste and we'll find your sweet spot.

Where in Bengaluru do you deliver?

We currently serve Bengaluru-wide via batched twice-weekly delivery. Members in our anchor apartment communities get free delivery; for other locations, a small delivery fee may apply depending on distance. Drop us a WhatsApp with your locality and we'll confirm.

How do I choose books? Do I have to browse or do you pick?

It's your call. Three ways to find your next read: (1) Browse our catalogue and pick exactly what you want. (2) Share your reading profile and let our curator recommend titles matched to your taste. (3) Mix the two - pick some, let us surprise you with the rest. Most members do all three over time.

What if you don't have a book I want?

Request it. Send us the title and author on WhatsApp, and we'll source most books within a week and add them to your next delivery. If a book is rare or out of print and we genuinely can't get it, we'll tell you upfront and suggest close alternatives. The catalogue grows because of what members ask for.

Can I see your catalogue before subscribing?

Yes. WhatsApp us and we'll share the full catalogue link. You can browse by genre, author, or reading level before deciding if mybookmark is right for you.

What if a book gets damaged?

Books get read - we expect some wear and tear, and that's built into our model. For minor damage (small marks, slight bending), no charge. For serious damage (torn pages, lost books, water damage), we charge 50% of the cover price. We tell you upfront so there are no surprises.

How does the refundable deposit work?

The deposit is held as security against the books in your possession. It's not income - it's protection. When you cancel, we audit the returned books and refund the deposit within 7 working days.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime. Quarterly and annual plans are pro-rated on cancellation with a small ₹500 admin fee. Your deposit is always returned in full.

Do you have books in languages other than English?

Currently our collection is primarily English, with a small Hindi and Kannada selection growing month by month. If you'd like books in a specific language, mention it during signup - your input shapes what we buy next.
