Do I need to pay a security deposit?

Yes, a refundable security deposit of Rs 10,000 is required at the time of delivery. It's fully refunded when you return the console in good condition.

What games are available?

Every rental comes with a PS Plus subscription, giving you access to a library of 100+ games including God of War Ragnarok, Spider-Man 2, Hogwarts Legacy, and more. No need to buy individual games.

Can I extend my rental?

Absolutely. Just reach out to us before your rental period ends and we'll extend it seamlessly. You can extend directly from your dashboard or contact our support team.

What if something breaks?

Normal wear and tear is on us. For accidental damage, the security deposit covers repairs. We'll walk you through the specifics at the time of delivery.

Where do you deliver?

All across Bengaluru. We deliver the PS5 to your doorstep anywhere in the city.

How does the security deposit refund work?

A refundable deposit of Rs 10,000 is collected at the time of delivery. Once you return the console in good condition, the full deposit is refunded within 2-3 business days.
