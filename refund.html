Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Our promise: Cancel anytime. No lock-in. No cancellation fees. Full deposit back.

We believe in a trust-first model. You should stay because you love the service, not because you're trapped in a contract.

1. Cancellation Policy

1.1 How to cancel

You can cancel your mybookmark subscription at any time through any of these channels:

  • WhatsApp: Message us at +91 70221 65307 saying you'd like to cancel
  • Email: Write to sales@mybookmark.in with your registered email

No forms to fill, no reason required, no retention calls. We process cancellations the same day.

1.2 What happens after cancellation

  1. We schedule a pickup for all books currently in your possession (typically within 2–3 business days)
  2. Our delivery agent collects the books from your doorstep
  3. We audit the returned books for condition (normal wear and tear is expected and accepted)
  4. Your full security deposit is refunded within 7 working days of the book audit

1.3 Mid-cycle cancellation

If you cancel in the middle of your billing cycle, your subscription remains active until the end of the current paid period. For example, if you paid on 1 August and cancel on 15 August, you can keep your books until 31 August. The remaining days are not prorated or refunded — your subscription simply ends at the cycle's close.

2.1 Deposit collection

  • The deposit is collected at the time of your first book delivery, along with the first month's subscription fee — not charged upfront online
  • Payment is accepted via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.)
  • The deposit is a security against the books in your possession. It is not a fee, not income, and not forfeited on cancellation

2.2 Full deposit refund

Your deposit is refunded in full when:

  • All borrowed books are returned
  • Books pass the condition audit (normal wear and tear is fine)
  • There are no outstanding subscription fees

Refund is processed via UPI transfer to the same account used for payment, within 7 working days of the audit.

2.3 Deductions from deposit

In rare cases, deductions may be made from the deposit before refund:

  • Damaged books: If a book is returned with significant damage (torn pages, water damage, broken spine, heavy stains, or missing pages), a charge of 50% of the book's cover price is deducted
  • Lost books: If a book cannot be returned, the same 50% of cover price charge applies
  • Outstanding fees: Any unpaid subscription fees at the time of cancellation are deducted

You will always be notified with details and photos before any deduction is made. You can dispute a damage assessment within 7 days of notification.

3. Subscription Fee Refunds

Monthly subscription fees are non-refundable once the billing cycle has started and books have been delivered. This is because the service (book access + delivery) has already been consumed.

  • If you have not received any books in a billing cycle due to our error (e.g., delivery failure on our end), a full refund of that month's fee will be issued
  • If there is a billing error or duplicate charge, contact us and we will correct it within 3 working days

4. PS5 Rental Refunds

  • Deposit: The ₹10,000 security deposit is fully refunded within 7 working days once the PS5 console and all accessories are returned in working condition
  • Rental fee: Non-refundable once the console has been delivered. If the console is defective upon delivery, we will replace it or refund the rental fee
  • Late returns: A charge of ₹500/day applies for returns past the rental period end date. This is deducted from the deposit
  • Damage: Repair or replacement costs for damaged console/controllers/accessories are assessed and deducted from the deposit

5. Delivery Charge Refunds

  • Delivery charges are non-refundable once the delivery has been completed
  • If a delivery fails due to our error (wrong address by us, courier issue), the delivery charge for that trip is waived
  • If you cancel a delivery request before the books are dispatched, no delivery charge applies

6. How to Request a Refund

For any refund queries or disputes:

WhatsApp: +91 70221 65307 (fastest response)

Email: sales@mybookmark.in

Response time: Within 2 hours during business hours (9 AM – 9 PM IST)

Refund processing: 7 working days via UPI
