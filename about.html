About mybookmark

A curated book subscription library with doorstep delivery in Bengaluru

Our Mission

To make reading accessible and affordable for every reader in Bengaluru. We believe books shouldn't collect dust on shelves — they should travel from hand to hand, sparking curiosity, joy, and knowledge along the way.

Who We Are

mybookmark is a subscription-based book library serving readers across Bengaluru. We curate a growing collection of 120+ titles across fiction, non-fiction, self-help, business, kids, and more — and deliver them right to your doorstep.

No bookstore trips. No Amazon impulse buys gathering dust. No library late fees. Just great books, delivered to your door, swapped whenever you're ready for the next one.

We also offer PS5 console rentals for gamers in Bengaluru — because entertainment shouldn't require ownership.

How It Works

  • Pick a plan — Kids (₹249/mo), Reader (₹549/mo), or Bookworm (₹849/mo)
  • Browse our catalogue — 120+ titles across all genres, updated regularly
  • Request books via WhatsApp — tell us what you want to read next
  • Books delivered to your door — every Wednesday across Bengaluru
  • Swap anytime — done reading? Swap for new titles. No due dates, no late fees
  • Cancel anytime — full deposit back, no questions asked
